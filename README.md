# Corporate Weather

Corporate Weather is a German-market layoff-risk signal scanner.

It analyzes public company signals such as restructuring language, hiring slowdown, employee sentiment, and German-specific terms like Sozialplan, Interessenausgleich, Stellenabbau, and Betriebsrat.

The app does not predict layoffs. It creates an explainable workplace weather report based on visible risk signals.

## MVP

User enters a company name and receives:

- Risk level: Clear, Watchlist, Cloudy, or Storm Warning
- Risk score from 0–100
- Confidence level
- Detected layoff-risk signals
- Missing evidence
- What to watch next
