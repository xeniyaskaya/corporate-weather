# Corporate Weather Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Corporate Weather analyzes visible public workplace-weather signals for companies in the DACH market. It is signal analysis only, not a prediction and not legal advice.

## Data We Process

Corporate Weather processes the company name that a user chooses to analyze. The app may also process the app interaction needed to render the report, such as the selected demo company or route inside the embedded view.

The app does not ask for precise location, payment details, government identifiers, health information, credentials, passwords, API keys, or full chat transcripts.

## Purpose

We use the company name only to generate a workplace-weather report with visible public signal categories such as hiring activity, public company communications, DACH business press indicators, and workplace review-style signals.

## Data Sources

The current app uses deterministic demo and simulated DACH signal analysis. It does not authenticate into user accounts, does not scrape private accounts, and does not access private company systems.

## Data Sharing

Corporate Weather does not sell personal data. The app is hosted through the deployed MCP/Skybridge infrastructure provider and may be processed by the hosting and platform services required to operate the app.

## Retention

Corporate Weather does not intentionally store user queries or generated reports in an application database. Platform and hosting providers may retain operational logs according to their own policies.

## User Controls

Users can disconnect the app from ChatGPT Apps settings. Users should avoid entering personal or sensitive information into the company search field.

## Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact the developer/publisher listed in the ChatGPT app submission profile.
